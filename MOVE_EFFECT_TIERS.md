# Move effect roles and tiers

All 258 effect folders in `move_VFX`, under the names on disk now.

**190 moves are in the game.** The unused effects below carry **45 more move ideas**: 40 that fit mechanics the game already has and 5 that need a new one. Several folders hold more than one idea (noted in the row).

| Tier | Meaning | Folders |
|---|---|---|
| S | In the game, as a move, part of a move, or a status or system effect | 176 |
| A | Not used. Move idea that fits existing mechanics | 40 |
| B | Not used. Move idea that needs a new mechanic | 5 |
| C | Not used. A part or marker, not a move on its own | 37 |
