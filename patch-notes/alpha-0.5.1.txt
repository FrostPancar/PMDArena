PMD: Dojo Battles
alpha 0.5.1
Patch size: XL

NEW MOVES (21)
Teleport, Heal Pulse, Dream Eater, Electroweb, Counter, Mud Shot, Blaze Kick,
Flare Blitz, Zap Cannon, Aurora Veil, Icy Wind, Spikes, Electro Ball, Venoshock,
Knock Off, Bone Rush, Double-Edge, Poison Powder, Solar Beam, Steel Wing, Iron Tail

MOVES AND PROGRESSION
- Moves are split into the ones a mon learns by itself and TMs.
- Natural moves unlock by level.
- A random TM is found after every fight. Any mon that can be taught it may use it.
- 36 existing moves added to mons that learn them naturally.
- Sticky Web holds the first foe to step in for 2 seconds, then disappears.

BALANCE AND FIXES
- Chill can no longer be applied to frozen foes.
- Detect now stuns the attacker it dodges.
- Fury Swipes no longer attacks twice on one use.
- Vine Whip pulls you and the foe to each other, or you to a wall.
- Double Team: no cast animation. The user's HP bar and arrow are hidden while
  copies are out. Copies show the user's stat changes and status.
- Dragon Dance is held, like Swords Dance, and raises Attack and Speed.
- Consecutive-use bonus (Fury Cutter and the like) scales less. The combo label
  sits above the move name.
- Stone Edge no longer leaves a pillar.
- Rock Wrecker leaves a pillar where it stops if it misses.
- CPUs no longer put Reflect on top of you.

INTERFACE
- Notches on the small energy bar over the player.
- Mobile: Run and Jump sit above the moves at the top right.
- Mobile: every move shows an aim guide while its button is held. Its colour is
  picked to stand out on each stage.
- Move menu: labelled icons for move kind, Speed instead of Range, a five-notch
  energy bar, TM discs tinted by type, NEW marks, padlocks from the item sheet.
- An evolution screen with congratulations after a fight that brings an evolution.
- The version is shown on the title screen.
