# PMD Arena: alpha 0.5.12

**Patch size: L**

Five new mons, a second form for Cubone, and critical hits.

## New mons
| Mon | Type | Evolves into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mimikyu | Ghost | none |
| Munchlax | Normal | Snorlax |
| Staryu | Water | Starmie |
| Honedge | Steel | Doublade, then Aegislash |
| Torkoal | Fire | none |

- **Cubone** now chooses between **Marowak** and **Alolan Marowak** when it is ready. Alolan Marowak is Fire type and brings fire and ghost moves.
- **Honedge** and Doublade slice for their close-range moves.
- **Staryu** learns Star Shoot and Hydro Burst.

## Critical hits
- Any hit has a 6% chance to land for 1.5x damage.
- **Slicing moves crit 25% of the time:** Cut, Slash, Night Slash, X-Scissor, Leaf Blade, Shadow Claw, Dragon Claw, Metal Claw, Razor Shell, Cross Poison, Steel Wing, Psycho Cut, Air Slash, Fury Cutter, Razor Leaf, Razor Wind, Aerial Ace.
- Beam and stream ticks and fixed-damage moves can't crit.

## Balance
- Close-range moves do 10% more damage.

## Moves
- **Kinesis** to Ralts.
- **Explosion** to Munchlax, Torkoal and Porygon.
- **Wood Hammer** to Mimikyu, Chikorita and Cacnea.
- **Eruption** and **Lava Plume** to Torkoal.
