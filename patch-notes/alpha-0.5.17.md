# PMD Arena: alpha 0.5.17

**Patch size: L**

Four new moves, reworked guards and grabs, and a long list of move changes.

## New moves
| Move | Type | What it does | Learned by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vice Grip | Normal | a grab: two hits, then both thrown apart | Corphish, Heracross, Scyther |
| King's Shield | Steel | a held guard; the first hit taken raises Attack for 8 s | Honedge |
| Safeguard | Normal | 8 s in which no status, stat drop or ill effect from a foe takes hold | Chikorita, Alolan Vulpix, Clefairy, Ralts, Staryu |
| Punishment | Dark | 50% harder for every stat the foe has raised | Zorua, Cacnea, Hawlucha |

## Guards and blocks
- **Iron Defense** has no charge time. The first hit taken while it is held sets off a blast all round you, a little wider than Shock Wave.
- **Bide** is now a held guard. The hit that ends it makes your next attack hit twice as hard.
- A **white flash** shows over an attack stopped by a shield, a substitute doll or a held guard.

## Grabs
- **Bite, the three Fangs, Bug Bite, Draining Kiss, Drain Punch and Vice Grip** land twice at half damage each. The foe is held reeling between the two hits, then both mons are thrown apart.

## Crits and luck
- **Focus Energy:** +25% critical chance for 8 s, and nothing else.
- **Lucky Chant:** Defense up, and no hit on you can be critical, for 8 s.
- **Acupressure** raises one of your stats at random.
- A spark shows over a critical hit.

## Move changes
- **Teleport moves** land where their guide is. On the pad that is as far as the move goes, the way the stick is pushed or the mon faces.
- **Shadow Sneak** uses its own art for each direction and reaches less far.
- **Flash Cannon** is one flash down a line to the spot you aim at.
- **Rock Blast** is a held stream, like Ember.
- **Will-O-Wisp** is a slow homing wisp.
- **Ingrain** lasts 10 s, heals 3 HP a second, and a jump ends it early.
- **Grass Knot** buries. **Wood Hammer** hits harder.
- **Sleep Talk** uses one of your other three moves, never Rest.
- **Attract** takes longer to charge. Held streams such as Bubble and Ember start at once.
- **Giga Impact** shows one impact, at the end of the dash.

## Looks
- New art for Cut, Fury Cutter (green), Bide and Taunt.
- A taunted mon has an anger mark over its head.
- A flare on each stack of Dragon Dance.
- Smaller rubble for Earthquake.
- A white ring round a snared mon.
- No aim guide while holding a move you steer (Dig, Surf, Brave Bird and the like).
