# PMD Arena: alpha 0.5.18

**Patch size: L**

Two new modes against the computer, and a new first question at the pillar.

## Story and Online
The pillar now asks **Story** or **Online** first.
- **Story:** 1v1, Crew, Survivor, Boss Rush.
- **Online:** fights against random players.

## Survivor
- A random stage and endless waves. Each wave is three mons at 25% HP, two at 50%, or one at 100%.
- The next wave comes in a second after the last mon falls.
- The CPUs go up a level every two waves, to level 5.
- Every fifth wave, a chest with a random TM.
- You get 20% of your health back for each wave beaten.
- EXP grows with the waves you beat, up to twice a normal fight at wave 15.

## Boss Rush
- Nine legendaries, met in a random order: Moltres, Zapdos, Articuno, Lugia, Ho-Oh, Mewtwo, Groudon, Kyogre, Rayquaza.
- Each has 500 HP, plays at level 4 and has its own stage.
- One round per fight. A win sends you straight to the next boss at full health.
- Each fight is worth 70% of the usual EXP.

## Moves
- **Protect** costs one notch, and holding it costs nothing more (up to 1.5 s). Used again straight after itself, it has a 30% chance to fail, then 60%, then 90%.
- **Light Screen:** your own shots that pass through it turn toward the nearest foe.
- **Rain Dance** halves Thunder's charge.
- **Waterfall** lands three falls in a triangle.
- **Gravity** shows grey orbs circling the zone.

## Looks
- Mons with their own Strike animation use it for close-range moves.
- Blaziken kicks, and slams for heavy moves.
