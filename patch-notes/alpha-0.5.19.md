# PMD Arena: alpha 0.5.19

**Patch size: M**

Traps get rules of their own, beam clashes ask for mashing outright, and a handful of moves look the way they should.

## Traps
- **No crits.** Spikes, Stealth Rock and Toxic Spikes never land a critical hit. Spikes always bites with the crit spark and sound anyway.
- **Costs.** Stealth Rock and Toxic Spikes cost three notches, Spikes two.
- **Layers.** Spikes thrown onto your own Spikes adds to that patch instead of laying a new one: radius 16, 22, 28 and damage 15, 22.5, 30, up to three layers. Each throw makes the patch good as new.

## Moves
- **Protect** has no charge time.
- **Incinerate** is now a short burst of flame out of the user toward the aim, with a 0.2 s charge.
- **Ingrain:** jumping pulls the roots up and jumps in the same press.
- **Beam clash:** once two beams lock, neither needs its button held, so the buttons are free to mash. The lock lands with the heavy-hit screen beat, and a MASH button hammers over your mon until it is settled.

## Looks
- **Shadow Sneak:** the shadow runs from where the user sank to where it rises, at any angle.
- **Electro Ball** carries a Thunder Shock spark inside it.
- **Petal Blizzard:** the petals sit at body height over the hit ring.
- **Dazzling Gleam** keeps its own colours and is smaller.
- **Tri Attack** and **Aurora Veil** charge with a burst of coloured rays.
- **Bosses** are smaller: 1.15x, down from 1.35x.
