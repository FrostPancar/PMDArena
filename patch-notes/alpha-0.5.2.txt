PMD: Dojo Battles
alpha 0.5.2
Patch size: M

GAME FEEL (visual only: nothing about how fights play has changed)
- A shot that is cut or beaten freezes in a ring, splits in two and fades.
- Every hit flashes its target and throws a burst of lines.
- The screen shakes on a hit, harder for stronger hits.
- Very heavy hits get a flash, a negative frame and a vignette.
- Beam clashes rumble for as long as they last.
- The camera trails dashes and glides after teleports.

ART
- Icy Wind uses Blizzard's hit effect.
- Iron Tail uses Dragon Rush's effect in grey.
- Knock Off uses Dragon Claw's effect.
- Poison Powder is a cleaner purple.
- Spikes has no purple left.
- Beams have a soft glow (not Mud Shot).
