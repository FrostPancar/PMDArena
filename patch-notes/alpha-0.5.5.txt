PMD: Dojo Battles
alpha 0.5.5
Patch size: M

(Also includes the loading fixes from 0.5.3 and 0.5.4.)

BALANCE
- Psychic is now a hold move. It lasts as long as you have the energy, and
  its field slowly follows your aim.
- Rest heals 40, down from 60.
- Healing moves are limited to 5 uses a match, shared between all of a mon's
  self-heals. Drains and Leech Seed do not count.
- Energy refills twice as fast.
- Beams no longer stagger the target or break its charge. Every beam slows
  what it hits, and hits twice as often for half the damage.

CONTROLS
- Moves are used when you let go. Holding a move's button or key shows the
  aim guide; releasing uses the move. Held moves still start on the press.
- R or C calls off a move you are aiming.
- Mobile: teleport moves go the way the stick is pushed, unless you drag the
  button to aim.
- Pressing B in the dojo opens a training floor with your current moveset.

FEEL
- A landed parry shakes the screen.

LOADING (0.5.3, 0.5.4)
- Move effects and mon sprites are loaded when they are needed, not all at
  start-up. This fixes the game running out of memory on iPhone.
- Fixed the choosing screen showing empty boxes.
