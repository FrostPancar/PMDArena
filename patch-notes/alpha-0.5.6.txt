PMD: Dojo Battles
alpha 0.5.6
Patch size: M

BURST MOVES
These now fire several shots one after another, each where you are aiming
at that moment. On keyboard and mouse one use sends them all; on the pad,
hold the button and let go to stop early.
- Bullet Seed: 5 seeds
- Swift: 5 stars
- Pin Missile: 4 needles (no longer cycles three different shots)
- Razor Leaf: 4 leaves, each still comes back
- Magical Leaf: 3 leaves

HELD STREAMS
These start on the press and keep going while the button is held, costing
energy as they go.
- Water Gun
- Bubble (no longer a three-way fan)
- Bubble Beam
- Ember
- Thunder Shock (small bolts where you aim; no lingering zone)
- Star Shoot
- Fury Swipes (swipe after swipe, each a little harder)

NEW MOVE
- Powder Snow: a short-range held stream with a small chance to freeze.
  Alolan Vulpix learns it naturally.

CHANGED MOVE
- Icy Wind is replaced by Dazzling Gleam: Fairy type, 18 damage, no slow.
  Alolan Vulpix learns it naturally; Clefairy and Ralts can be taught it.
