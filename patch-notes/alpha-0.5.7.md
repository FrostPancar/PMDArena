# PMD Arena: alpha 0.5.7

**Patch size: L**

A balance and behaviour pass over about forty moves.

## Streams, lobs and beams
- **Held streams** (Water Gun, Bubble, Bubble Beam, Ember, Star Shoot, Powder Snow, Thunder Shock) no longer stagger. No more stun locks.
- **Lobbed moves** burst 50% wider. They deal 75% damage up close and up to 150% at full range.
- **Aurora Beam** is now a short-range held stream of rings.
- **Hypnosis** fires two rings back to back, at shorter range.
- **Ice Beam** has diamond particles.

## Costs and charges
| Move | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Earthquake | costs 2 |
| Hurricane | costs 4 |
| Outrage, Uproar | cost 4, can't be reused while one is going |
| Eruption, Hammer Arm | charge faster |

- Area moves with no charged build-up come out at once.
- Charged area moves grow faster and wider. Earthquake is unchanged.

## Hold moves
- You can **jump** during Surf, Ice Ball, Rollout and Gyro Ball.
- **Armour:** Brave Bird, Rollout, Ice Ball and Gyro Ball still take damage, but can't be knocked back or interrupted.
- **Gyro Ball** is a hold move like Ice Ball.
- **Protect** is held for energy and no longer slides across.
- **Dig:** only Earthquake and Magnitude reach a mon underground.

## Moves
- **Zap Cannon** and **Sleep Powder** home in.
- **Fire Blast** bursts twice where it ends.
- **Circle Throw** always throws behind you, much further.
- **Acrobatics** springs off on a hit in the direction you hold.
- **Rain Dance** powers up your side's water moves for 10 s.
- **Bide** and **Counter:** take a hit during them and your next attack is empowered.
- **Attack Order** summons a 1 HP copy of you that uses Pin Missile and Stun Spore.
- **Petal Blizzard** sits higher and lingers for 4 s.
- **Hurricane** and **Kinesis** levitate the target. No second lift for 2 s after landing.
- **Gravity** is a much larger grey zone that stops jumps.
- **Future Sight** and **Waterfall** hit a much bigger area.
- A mon that digs out of a burial can't be buried again for 2 s.

## Looks
- Lock-On's reticle follows its target.
- All heals use Leech Seed's heal animation.
- Venoshock is purple.
- Roost charges with the Sleep animation.
- Torment shows the red cross overhead, like Disable.
