# PMD Arena: alpha 0.5.9

**Patch size: L**

The game has sound now, plus two new fighting rules.

## Music
- Every stage and screen has its own track, from Red Rescue Team and Explorers.
- Tracks loop from their loop point, not from the intro.
- **Versus Legendary** plays in fights against level 5 CPUs.
- Jingles for a loss, a stage opening, a TM found, a level up and an evolution.

## Sound effects (first pass)
- Hits, jumps, parries, clashes, faints and heals.
- 180 of the 252 moves make a sound when used.
- Menus tick as you step through them.

## Sound settings
- **Music** and **Sounds** switches in the pause menu.
- A **Sound** tab in the top right of every room, for phones.
- **M** mutes the music on a keyboard.

## Fighting
- **Clash:** when two close-range blows meet, neither lands. Both mons are thrown apart and reel briefly. Heavy moves go through lighter ones.
- **Ice pillar:** Icicle Crash leaves a spire of ice where it lands. It is cover like a rock, but any attack chips it and it breaks.
- **Heavy hits** knock back again as well as stunning.
- **Tadpole Pond:** you can hop between the side pools at the top and the bank.

## Looks
- New title card.
- **Spikes** are caltrops that stay visible on the ground.
- **Ember** is a fireball that faces the way it flies.
- New bursts of light for a landed parry and for a shot that is cut out of the air.
